# Excel-to-Word Handoff

Use this checklist after analyzing the workbook:

- [ ] State the recommended channel and pack size.
- [ ] Cite the exact workbook metric or comparison supporting the recommendation.
- [ ] Distinguish unit lift from net revenue and distribution effects.
- [ ] Name one regional or discount caveat.
- [ ] Define one post-launch measure and an adjustment trigger.
- [ ] Move only validated findings into the Word decision brief.
