# Research basis

Prepared 2026-09-18 for the MS-4018 learning package.

## Microsoft Learn objective alignment

The public MS-4018 module framing supplied for this build centers on two complementary behaviors: using AI to draft and improve impactful business documents, and using AI to interrogate structured data for patterns, comparisons, trends, and decisions. The package therefore makes the Word artifact the business question and communication surface, and the Excel artifact the evidence and exploration surface.

## Cola beverage workflow alignment

Public beverage and food-retail operating patterns commonly connect retailer promotion requests with demand planning, pack-size and channel mix, regional execution, distribution availability, price/discount guardrails, and post-promotion measurement. The synthetic workbook mirrors those workflow dimensions without copying any real company's data or brand assets.

## Design decisions

- Commercial Planning Manager is the bridge role between account request, analysis, and leadership decision.
- The handoff requires evidence plus a caveat, preventing a simple volume-only recommendation.
- The scenario uses Best For You Organics Company exactly as listed in the installed approved company CSV.
- The scenario uses Olivia Wilson, Amin Alimov, and Daniela Petkovska from the installed approved names CSV.
- All dates and metrics are fabricated for training and labeled synthetic.
