# MS-4018 Cola Industry Demo

A public-safe, synthetic demo package connecting the MS-4018 modules **Draft impactful documents using AI** and **Uncover new data insights with AI**.

## Scenario

Learners act as Olivia Wilson, Commercial Planning Manager at the approved fictitious company Best For You Organics Company. They turn a retailer promotion request into a Word decision brief, test the recommendation in Excel, and bring verified evidence back into Word.

## Delivery paths

- **30-minute quick demo:** presenter-led Word → Excel → Word flow.
- **60-minute hands-on lab:** learners draft, analyze, chart, transfer findings, and peer review.

## Contents

- `demo/DEMO-INSTRUCTIONS.docx` and `.md` — presenter flow.
- `demo/sample-data/01-Retailer-Promotion-Request.docx` — source packet.
- `demo/sample-data/02-Summer-Cola-Promotion-Decision-Brief.docx` — working Word brief.
- `demo/sample-data/03-Cola-Promotion-Insights.xlsx` — synthetic Excel workbook.
- `demo/sample-data/04-Excel-to-Word-Handoff.md` — handoff checklist.
- `research-notes.md` — public research basis and design choices.

## Data safety

All names, dates, metrics, products, channels, regions, and events are synthetic. No real brand, customer, retailer, employee, or confidential information is used. Company and person identities come from the installed approved demo data skill.
