# MS-4018 Cola Promotion Demo

## Storyline

Olivia Wilson, Commercial Planning Manager at Best For You Organics Company, uses Copilot in Word to shape a retailer request into a summer cola promotion decision brief. She then uses Copilot in Excel to examine synthetic sales, promotion, channel, pack-size, and regional patterns. Verified findings return to Word as the evidence, risks, and next actions in the recommendation.

## 30-minute quick demo

1. Frame the request and audience (5 min).
2. Draft and tighten the Word recommendation with Copilot (7 min).
3. Analyze Excel channel, pack, region, and discount signals (10 min).
4. Return to Word and update evidence and recommendation (6 min).
5. Close with one post-launch measure (2 min).

## 60-minute hands-on lab

Learners define the decision question, draft the brief, analyze the workbook, create or refine a chart, transfer three verified insights into Word, and peer-review evidence, caveats, and next action.
